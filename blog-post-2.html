Embed BI Dashboards Securely on Your Site

Published 2025-08-10 • 6 min read

Embedding BI dashboards can be fast and secure when done right. Here are the main options.

Power BI

  • Publish to web (public): easy but not secure. Avoid for sensitive data.
  • Secure embed with Azure AD: recommended for internal apps/sites.

Tableau

  • Tableau Public: public only
  • Server/Cloud with trusted tickets or OAuth: secure
